    Selecting and modifying

    This chapter will describe how elements are selected and changed.

    Being written

    This chapter is completed with area R2 of BIMopen's realignment, Selection and modify: each feature is compared with the reference behaviour, corrected where needed and checked through the interface, and only then described here. Until then, this page lists what it will cover.

    What this chapter will cover

    • highlighting under the cursor, Tab to cycle between overlapping elements and chains of walls;
    • selection windows from left to right and from right to left, Ctrl and Shift;
    • filtering a selection and Select All Instances;
    • dragging elements in every view: joined walls stretch, hosted elements follow;
    • the arrow keys, temporary dimensions, pinning;
    • the tools of the Modify panel: Move, Copy, Rotate, Mirror, Array, Align, Offset, Trim/Extend, Split Element, Delete;
    • undo and redo.

    Meanwhile

    The interface summarises selection, and Keyboard shortcuts lists the keys and the typed shortcuts of the Modify tools. At the command line, help followed by the name of a command shows its usage.
