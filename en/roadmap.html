Roadmap

Where we are, where we are going

BIMopen covers, command by command, the features of professional BIM software for architecture. Every feature was compared option by option with the expected behaviour: the steps, in order of priority, and the coverage percentages come from there. A step counts as done only when its commands have been tried in the interface, on the desktop and in the browser, the way a designer uses them, and are covered by automated interface tests.

  4. R4

    Floors, roofs, stairs and curtain walls

    Aligned and tested: sketch mode for floors and roofs with picked walls, openings, slope arrows and slope lines, and edit boundary; the profile of walls and curtain walls sketched in a section or elevation; stairs by run (with guides for the next run) or by boundary and riser lines, spiral ones too, and ramps, railings and components from the library, with the preview following the cursor. Curtain walls were aligned with R3. Monolithic runs join their landings with no step in the soffit, and railings run on over the landings as one rail. Spiral stairs and curved rails are smooth surfaces, with no lines between facets in 3D, hidden line, sections and elevations; railings have a post at every plan corner and every change of slope, on ramps too, and go round landings with acute angles without crossing. Sketch lines are selected and changed like other lines: ends dragged, Move, Offset, Trim/Extend, temporary dimensions. Wall layers and core boundaries are chosen with TAB as references for Align and Pick Lines.

    Ready: Stair, Railing, Object. Behaviours verified: 237 of 251.

    In progress

Sun and shadows in plans, sections, elevations and 3D, with the sun path and the sun study.
