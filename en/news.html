0.1.0-alpha.8 2026-11-09

Latest version Download

Highlights

All changes 15

Added

  • Spell checker for text notes («F7»), with its settings in Options, and Find and Replace by group type.
  • Legends with samples of materials and line styles that follow what they show, the subcategories of doors, windows, stairs and railings, and colour fill legends in sections and elevations.
  • Arrow types of the project, the font, width and reading of dimension text, alternate units, and the spot elevation's symbol and leader arrow.
  • Formatted text notes: bold, italic, underline, superscript, subscript, all caps, lists and indents; leaders that snap to the notes nearby and run under the text.
  • Locked tangencies in sketches and elliptical walls with their controls in plans and 3D views.
  • Free 2D views of the types «Detail» and «Scheme», and views and 2D elements saved to a new file and inserted from a file.
  • Loaded tags with a tag type for every category, the multi-category tag as a category's default, numbering that goes on from the last mark typed, and the tag's elbow and free end in every view.
  • Keynotes for elements, materials and free notes, from the project's table or a linked file, with their tree, settings and legend.
  • The stair calculator: calculation rules with their multipliers, the live result and a preview.
  • Graphic schedules of doors, windows and curtain walls, by type or by element, also on sheets.
  • The section box of 3D views, the orthographic 3D view and the orientation cube's full menu, section and elevation types, elevations placed with one click, the Project Index organised by the user, and the crop region of 3D views.
  • Recent files in the browser version, listed with their picture, and recent components under «Components».

Changed

  • The orientation cube has light faces and a compass ring with the cardinal points in the interface's language.
  • Dialogs in Arabic read from right to left.
  • Stair landings follow the sides of their runs until they meet.

0.1.0-alpha.7 2026-11-01

Download

Highlights

All changes 27

Added

  • Live view and walk mode: a realistic view beside the 3D view, with the time of day; the model walked through with the keys and the mouse, at eye height, up the stairs and through the doors.
  • Camera views: «Perspective view» placed with two clicks in plans, sections and elevations, its camera with grips, its crop region and far clip plane; on a sheet a camera view shows its perspective in its crop region.
  • Walkthroughs: a path drawn in a plan becomes a moving camera view, its key frames edited, played in its view and exported as images; key frames and recordings made while walking become walkthroughs.
  • USD export and project bundles: the 3D view exported as a USD scene with its materials, properties, camera and sun; a project gathered into one .zip with its linked models and the files they use, and opened back.
  • Ceilings with their reflected ceiling plans, architectural and structural columns, and curved walls: circles, arcs and fillets in the wall's «Draw» panel, a curved wall being one smooth wall with all its layers.
  • Copy, Cut and Paste, also aligned onto chosen levels and views; model and detail groups; «Transfer type».
  • DWG and DXF: views and sheets exported with a layer per category; drawings imported into views or linked.
  • Sheets and documentation: detail and sheet numbers in view heads and titles, text, lines and dimensions drawn on sheets, the four elevations in the template, the axonometric 3D view, and stair tread and riser numbers.
  • Phases and demolition: existing, demolished, new and temporary work; the «Phases» dialog with phase filters and their graphics; the phase of every view; «Demolish» in every view; infills; rooms and schedules by phase; phases written to IFC and DWG.
  • Openings and shafts: openings by face, vertical, in walls and for dormers, and shafts through several levels with their symbol lines, cutting floors, roofs and ceilings in plans, sections and 3D views.
  • Site and terrain: terrains from a sketch, points or files, with contours; building pads, sub-areas, split and merged terrains, graded regions with cut and fill, property lines drawn or from a table of distances and bearings, contour labels, the site plan, and trees, parking spaces and other site components standing on the terrain.
  • Structure: beams, braces, beam systems, trusses, structural walls and floors, isolated, wall and slab foundations, with their tags, end controls and rebar covers.
  • Design options: option sets, editing an option, views and schedules of one option, «Make primary» and «Accept primary».
  • Multi-category and material tags and tags with several hosts; diameter, radius and arc length dimensions; casework; dormers, extrusion roofs, sloped glazing and soffits; 3D lines and 3D text on a work plane; splines and ellipses edited by their controls.
  • Every schedule edits the model as the wall schedule does: a change in the table changes the elements, and a change of type is asked first.
  • Whole walls dimensioned in one click with their doors and windows, in plans, sections and elevations.

Changed

  • The existing commands realigned, item by item, with the main reference features: selection and the Modify tools (Align, Array with linked arrays, Shift in every tool), walls and columns, floors, roofs and ceilings, stairs, ramps and railings, the «Draw» panel (centre mark, tangent arcs from walls, fillets) and the view properties.
  • Every dialog has one look: the title bar, the message with its icon and the buttons in a band at the foot.
  • The Project Index lists the components by category, then component, then type.
  • New plans, sections and elevations start as line drawings; levels and grids start hidden in 3D views.
  • File dialogs no longer block the window, and the window opens centred.

Fixed

  • Walls nearly in line meet on the plane between them.
  • Walls lower than the room computation height no longer bound the room.
  • Constraints never move a pinned element.
  • Room tags in sections stand where the section cuts the room.
  • A 3D view's thumbnail in the views' strip is the picture of its window.
  • Edges found on the screen are drawn on every graphics card.

0.1.0-alpha.6 2026-10-26

Download

Highlights

All changes 22

Added

  • The native file format 2027: projects (.bpr), project templates (.bpt), components (.bco) and component templates (.bct); every file written in format 2027 or later opens in every later version.
  • The File panel: a full-window page in place of the File menu, with the recent projects as tiles showing the picture saved in each file, a list view, pinned projects, a file that is no longer there greyed with «Locate…», and the pages of New, Open, Save as, Export and Print.
  • Imperial units: feet and fractional inches, decimal feet and inches, ft² and ft³, values typed as 5'-6 1/2", architectural and engineering scales, and an imperial template for new projects.
  • «Import IFC»: an IFC file brought into the open project, its elements converted with the project's own types; in the File panel under «Open».
  • Imported objects, from IFC files too, can be converted into native elements: walls, floors, roofs and the other categories with the project's own types, or components built from their shapes, keeping the originals; the «Conversion Status» panel shows what is still to convert.
  • Stairs drawn by their boundary and riser lines: each tread and landing as the lines draw it, with nosings, middle stringers and a stepped underside.
  • The «Realistic» visual style: a physical sky with the sun's disc, golden hour, twilight and night, the moon in its phase and the stars, and moonlight on the model.
  • The «Pastel» visual style: a wash drawing in the colours of the materials, in 3D views, plans, sections and elevations, with the cuts in poché and the view's own shadows, on light or dark paper; «Wash Strength» and «Wash Variation» set how deep and how unevenly the colour lies.
  • Sketchy lines: the lines of a view drawn as by hand, with «Distortion» and «Extension» (5 and 1 in plans, sections and elevations, 8 and 3 in 3D views), the same on screen, in print and in export, turned on and off from the view bar.
  • «Graphic Display Options» in groups that open and close, with distortion, edges, smooth lines, transparency, depth cueing and the background gradient.
  • Options ▸ Graphics: hardware acceleration, the graphics card to use and the one «In use», and the kind of edges in 3D views.
  • Updates: at start, at most once a day, BIMopen reads the latest version from the website; a bell with a dot in the top bar shows a newer one, with its news, «Download and update», «Later» and «Skip this version». The download is checked (checksum and digital signature) and the program restarts on the same project and view; Android opens the system installer, the web reloads. Options ▸ General ▸ «Check for updates».
  • «?» ▸ «Commands»: every command in a window, with search, the ribbon's tabs, the name to type, synonyms, shortcut, description and an example; a double-click or Enter starts it. Typing help opens it too.

Changed

  • A new identity: the cube mark and the program's colours in the program, its icons, the website and the manual.
  • The «BIMopen» style is more compact: the rail lists the ribbon's tabs and the drawing area has more room.
  • «?» ▸ «About BIMopen» shows the build's version, date and commit, the platform, links to the website, the manual and the news, the third-party licences, and «Copy system information».
  • Large models open, draw and change faster in 3D views, with their many doors, windows, components, railing posts and imported objects.

Fixed

  • In 3D views automatic edges stay whole close up, and a visual style looks the same however it was chosen.
  • «Realistic» in browsers with WebGL2: walls and roofs no longer turn black, and the window no longer freezes while the images are prepared.
  • A graphics card that cannot draw in the window gives way to the automatic one at start.
  • In a stair drawn by runs each run meets its landing across the slab, and the landing post stays on the landing.

Removed

  • The experimental import of a closed format: BIMopen exchanges models through IFC.

0.1.0-alpha.5 2026-10-20

Download

Highlights

All changes 34

Added

  • A «Reinforced concrete» ribbon tab between Structure and Metal with the reinforcement commands, in both interface styles and in touch mode.
  • Sun path aids in every view («Visual Aids»): hour lines, solar noon, the year's band between the solstices, analemmas, sunrise and sunset with their times; in 3D views the model hides the part of the path behind it.
  • The sun dragged in 3D views as in plans, «Along the Day» or «Along the Year»; sunrise, sunset and the length of the day in the view bar; the year's sunrises and sunsets as a chart in «Sun Settings», where a click picks the day.
  • Summer time follows the rule of each place and the sun's year by itself; Tokyo, São Paulo, Santiago, Sydney and Auckland join the city list.
  • Railing types with «Rail Structure (Non-Continuous)» and «Baluster Placement» (main pattern, posts at the start, corners and end), each with a preview on a sample stair.
  • «Railing ▸ Place on a host» in plans and 3D views, with «Offset from Path» and «On treads» or «On stringer».
  • The complete «Snap» dialog, the same in both styles: length and angular increments chosen by the zoom, object snaps with their two-letter overrides, «Restore Defaults»; the settings are kept with the preferences.
  • Angle snaps in every view: a segment snaps to horizontal, vertical, parallel, perpendicular or a multiple of the angular increment, measured from the view's axis or from the previous segment, with a guide and the angle shown; in plans, sections, elevations, 3D views and sketches, also for Rotate and radial arrays.
  • «Snap Overrides» in every view where a tool takes points, typed or from the right-button menu; TAB and SHIFT+TAB go through the snaps under the cursor; «Close» (SZ) closes a chain or a sketch's loop.
  • Every ribbon drop-down lists all its ways, also found by the command search; the ways still to come are greyed as «Not available yet».
  • Options bar: «Load Component» for doors, windows and components, «Tag on Placement» as a checkbox, «Rotate after inserting» for components, the wall's offset.
  • snap and anglesnap at the command line and through MCP.
  • In the user manual pictures open large on a click; the PDF has a cover.
  • «Keep coincident lines» in «Print Setup» prints every overlapping line instead of merging them.

Changed

  • Dragging the sun is smooth in every view: plans, sections and elevations show a draft shadow while it moves and the full one once released; a sun study plays the same way.
  • Shadows in 3D views have readable edges and smooth soft borders; the sunlit jamb of a window or door is lighter than the one turned away.
  • Every dialog opens in the middle of the screen each time and keeps one size while open; what does not fit scrolls inside it.
  • City names of the project location are shown in the interface language; files keep their location.
  • Printed and exported sections and elevations (PDF, SVG, PNG, image export) remove hidden lines exactly.
  • Sections and elevations paint overlapping faces in their true depth order, and window glass hides what is behind it.
  • «On stringer» is offered only on a stair with a stringer on that side; «Place on a host» adds only the railing a side is missing.
  • The sample house stands in Cagliari, where its address is; new projects still start in Rome.
  • Dialogs built around long lists (materials, visibility and graphics, object styles, filters, line styles, schedule properties, parameters, keyboard shortcuts…) are resized by their edges and open again at that size; their content never resizes them.
  • The sample house's ground floor is one continuous wall with its shop front embedded in it, as a curtain wall cuts the wall it runs along.

Fixed

  • A basic wall changed to a stacked type keeps its location line: the façade no longer moves by half its thickness.
  • Each sub-wall of a stacked wall joins the wall beside it at its own height, with the layers closed round the corner.
  • Opening a project over another shows the new one in every view; every way of opening asks to save changes; saving a 3D view under a new name no longer moves the open view's camera.
  • Railings on stairs run on the stair's outline, posts stand whole on the steps, balusters are evenly spaced and rails no longer sink into the first tread.
  • Window frames no longer vanish in elevations where the sill shares their plane.
  • A face grazed by the sun is evenly lit, without dark streaks in 3D views and elevations.
  • The sun path's analemma is one smooth loop through the sun, and in perspective its lines are cut behind the eye.
  • Answering «No» to saving and then opening the same file reloads it as it was saved.
  • In 3D views the angle snap guide lies on the wall being drawn.
  • A wall's area, in schedules, material takeoffs and IFC quantities, leaves out the opening of an embedded curtain wall.

0.1.0-alpha.4 2026-10-19

Download

Highlights

All changes 29

Added

  • A start screen while the program loads, with the real loading stages, in the saved language, else the system's, else English; it can be turned off in Options.
  • Stacked walls: sub-walls drawn in every view with their own layers, set in «Type sub-walls», picked one by one with Tab and split with «Break up into single walls».
  • Sun and shadows in plans, sections and elevations as in 3D views, with the sun path, «Sun Settings» and the sun study; one sun for the project or one for a view.
  • Temporary dimensions on every element in every view, 3D included, with witness lines anchored to any reference and remembered, and the «Temporary Dimensions» settings.
  • Touch mode for touch screens and pens, with two-finger gestures and layouts for tablets and phones.
  • Drawing in 3D views: walls, floors, roofs and sketches with the same preview, guides and temporary dimensions as in plan, and the Modify tools on sketch lines and elements.
  • Work planes and reference planes.
  • «Type layers»: the layers of a type as cards, with total thickness, thermal resistance, mass per area and a live preview.
  • Every ribbon tab complete: 75 commands still to come are shown greyed as «Not available yet», ten new panels among them.
  • A view dragged out of the drawing area opens in a window of its own, on another monitor too.
  • The strip of views and components with live thumbnails, and dockable Properties and Project Index panels with a type selector that shows previews.

Changed

  • The wall's Properties panel and «Type Parameters» in groups, with derived values read only and rows greyed while another value does not allow them.
  • Every dialog with a preview has a «Preview» switch in its header and keeps one size and place.
  • The Classic style is chosen in Options beside the BIMopen one, without a separate option to enable it.
  • The Classic style gets a new title bar, a ribbon that scrolls instead of cutting buttons off, one options bar and no status bar.
  • The main window fills the screen at the first start; later it opens as it was closed.
  • The orientation cube has bevelled facets and arrows around it, and more contrast on light and dark backgrounds.
  • Hidden line keeps white faces and dark lines in both themes; the shaded styles fill plans and sections with the materials' colours.
  • Colours and patterns come from the material alone, in every view and every preview.
  • Stair and ramp railings follow the stair's boundary on each side from the start; curved stairs and rails are smooth surfaces.
  • Functions not available yet are greyed in both interface styles.
  • The user manual shows the BIMopen style; its «Interface styles» chapter shows the Classic style and touch mode on a monitor, a tablet and a phone.

Fixed

  • The 3D view draws walls, floors, roofs and frames without hairline cracks.
  • Door and window frames no longer overlap at their corners: no flicker in 3D, no stray lines in sections and elevations.
  • The first-floor slab of the sample house stops at the walls' core, behind the insulation.
  • No spikes at the edges of the orientation cube.
  • On Wayland the main window no longer closes after the start screen.
  • A panel made floating is no longer drawn twice.
  • Openings keep their wall at corners and when copied; a click on a window's glass picks the window in sections, elevations and 3D.

0.1.0-alpha.1 2026-10-15 The first test version: model, views, PDF sheets, IFC. 10

Added

  • First test builds for Windows 10/11 (64 bit) and Linux (x86_64), next to the web app.
  • Modelling of levels, grids, layered walls, doors, windows, floors, roofs, stairs, railings, curtain walls and rooms, with plans, sections, elevations, 3D views, schedules, sheets printed to PDF and IFC4 export.
  • IFC import (IFC2x3 and IFC4): every product with a body is shown, with its faces correctly oriented, open or mixed-winding shells included.
  • Orientation cube with 26 clickable regions, a compass ring at its base, arrows when a face is seen straight on, and a menu with its options.
  • Framing elevations.
  • Annotation block schedule: a schedule of the symbols of one component.
  • Two-letter keyboard shortcuts, such as WA for the wall, act as soon as they are typed in the drawing area; shared shortcuts can be exported and imported as XML.
  • The right mouse button opens the menu of the running tool, with snap overrides; the overrides can also be typed for the next point (SE, SM, SI, SN, SO).

Changed

  • One orbit for every 3D navigation: the drawing area, the orientation cube and the mouse wheel turn the view the same way.

Fixed

  • Elevation and section fills are drawn inside their outlines, without spikes or fills over the whole outline.
