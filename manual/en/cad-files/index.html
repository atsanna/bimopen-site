    DWG and DXF

    BIMopen writes and reads AutoCAD drawings, DWG and DXF, of every release from 2000 to 2018. A view or a sheet goes out as a drawing with a layer per category; a drawing comes in as a CAD file that stays one element of the project, imported or linked.

    Exporting a view or a sheet

    File ▸ Export ▸ DWG / DXF… opens Export CAD Formats:

    Option What it does
    Name The file's name, the view's or sheet's by default
    Current view or sheet / Selected views/sheets The open view or sheet, or those ticked with Select… (a file each, named after them)
    Format DWG or DXF
    Version AutoCAD 2000, 2004, 2007, 2010, 2013 or 2018
    One DWG unit is millimetre (the default), centimetre, metre, inch or foot
    Layers and properties Category on layer, overrides by entity (the default: a colour other than the layer's stays on the object), All on layer, no overrides, or All on layer, new layers for overrides (a layer NAME-RRGGBB for each other colour)
    Colours RGB as in the view, or Index colour (255): the nearest colour of the 255-colour table
    Coordinates Project Internal, or Shared: plans and views with their origin at the survey point and their axes on true north (only when the project has shared coordinates; sheets keep paper coordinates)
    Naming Automatic: short (the view's name) or Automatic: long (the project's name before the view's)

    What the view draws goes out: cut walls and the other elements as closed polylines with their solid fill, lines, arcs, circles and texts (dimensions and tags as texts and lines). Each element goes on the layer of its category, after the AIA names (A-WALL, A-DOOR, A-GLAZ, A-FLOR, S-COLS, S-GRID, A-ANNO-DIMS, A-ANNO-TEXT…), fills on the same name with -PATT; the colours are the ones of the view. Plans, sections, elevations, drafting views and sheets can be exported. On the web the file is downloaded.

    The choices stay for the next export of the same session.

    At the command line: export pianta.dwg {"version":"2013","unit":"mm","layers":"bylayer","colors":"aci","coordinates":"shared"}.

    Importing or linking a drawing

    Insert ▸ Import CAD and Insert ▸ Link CAD (in the BIMopen style: Import CAD… and Link CAD… in the File panel and among the commands) ask for a DWG or DXF, then show its options:

    Option What it does
    Current view only The drawing belongs to the open view only (a plan, a section, a 2D view)
    Colours Invert (light lines drawn dark: for drawings made on a black background), Preserve, Black and white
    Layers All, Visible (the layers off in the file are left out), or Specify…: the layers to import are ticked
    Import units Auto-detect (the unit the file declares, else millimetres), mm, cm, dm, m, inches, feet, or Custom: how many metres one unit of the file is
    Positioning Auto – Center to center (the drawing's centre on the centre of the level's walls), Auto – Origin to origin, or Auto – By shared coordinates (the drawing's origin on the survey point, its axes on true north)
    Place at The level whose plans show the drawing
    Correct lines that are slightly off axis On (the default): lines within 0.1° of the axes become horizontal or vertical
    Orient to View On (the default): the drawing follows the view's axes; off, its north is the project's true north

    What is read: lines, polylines with their arcs, arcs, circles, ellipses, splines, texts, solid hatches and blocks (expanded) of model space; heights are ignored, the drawing is 2D.

    The CAD file in the project

    A CAD file is one element, named after its file: it is selected, moved, turned, copied and deleted as the others, and its lines are snap points. It is drawn in the plans of its level, or only in its view. Its Properties show its level, its colours and, linked, its path, and its layers, each with a tick that shows or hides it.

    The contextual panel Import Instance:

    • Explode (imports only): its lines, texts and solid hatches become detail lines, text notes and filled regions of the open view;
    • Delete Layers: the layers ticked leave the project with their lines;
    • Reload and Manage Links (links only).

    Properties also change the drawing's Scale Factor and Rotation. Explode takes drawings of up to 10,000 objects.

    Manage Links

    Insert ▸ Manage Links lists the linked CAD files with their state (Loaded, Not loaded, Not found), their saved path and their objects. Reload reads the file again from its path (the hidden layers and the position stay), Reload From… takes another file, Unload keeps the link without drawing it, Import turns it into an import and Remove deletes it. On the web there is no path: Reload asks for the file again.

    A linked drawing is kept in the project too: the project opens without the file.

    The list also shows each file's size.
